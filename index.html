Renderer notes

Geometry is prepared once, pipelines are cached, and repeated meshes share instanced draws. All five core glTF material textures are supported: base color, metallic/roughness, normal, occlusion, and emissive. Lighting uses a directional light with a small ambient term, plus environment diffuse light and roughness-dependent reflections. Open an equirectangular PNG/JPEG panorama or use the built-in studio environment. Animation, linear blend skinning, and morph targets are supported. Four-sample MSAA smooths geometry edges before tone mapping.
